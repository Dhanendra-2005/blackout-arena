Match rules

The short version — full ruleset is sent with your room ID.

Scoring
Placement points follow standard Booyah League scoring (1st: 15, 2nd: 12, down to 10th: 1). Each kill is worth 1 point. Ties are broken by total kills, then by earliest match completion.
Fair play
No emulators in mobile-only brackets, no teaming with rival squads, no exploiting map glitches. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours with screenshot evidence from both sides.
No-shows
Squads not in the custom room within 5 minutes of room ID release forfeit that match. No refunds for no-shows.
